## What Do You Want to Learn & Contribute?

### What do you hope to learn from this internship?

- I hope to experience what it is like to work as part of a real development team in a production-like environment.
- I want to learn how developers collaborate with each other and interact with other teams while working on a shared product.
- I want to gain practical experience with debugging, testing, reviewing changes, and checking things thoroughly before considering work complete.
- I also want to understand how deployment and other parts of the development process work in a real team.
- Since I have limited experience with backend development in a real-world environment, I want to understand backend technologies more deeply and learn how different parts of a codebase and repository work together.

### What skills are you most excited to develop?

- Collaborating effectively with other developers and teams while working on my own part of a larger codebase.
- Improving my backend development skills and understanding the technology stack in more depth.
- Becoming more comfortable with Git, Git commands, repositories, branching, and integrating changes.
- Understanding how different components of an application work together in a real project.
- Exploring Focus Bear as both a developer and a user, including looking at the application from a consumer's perspective and identifying potential issues or vulnerabilities.

### What do you think you can contribute to the team?

- I hope to contribute by applying what I know, bringing new ideas, and finding practical solutions to the problems I work on.
- I can contribute by researching issues, suggesting solutions, debugging problems, and helping improve the codebase.
- I would also like to contribute to backend development tasks and take responsibility for the work assigned to me.

### What is one area you feel less confident in that you'd like to improve?

- I have limited experience working on a small part of a large codebase that is connected to work being done by multiple teams.
- I am also less confident with real-world development workflows such as integrating changes across teams and deploying applications.
- I would like to become more comfortable with these processes by working with them practically during the internship.

### What does success in this internship look like for you?

- Success would mean that both the Focus Bear team and I are satisfied with my contribution to the product.
- I want to make meaningful contributions to the application while learning from the work I am given.
- I would like to finish the internship with a much deeper understanding of backend development, the technology stack, Git and repository workflows, debugging, collaboration, and deployment.
- Most importantly, I want to understand not just how to complete individual tasks, but how software development works as part of a real team and product.
